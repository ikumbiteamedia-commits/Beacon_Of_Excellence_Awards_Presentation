What exactly are we building?

Beacon is a recognition and visibility platform designed to discover people and organisations doing meaningful work, give that excellence a professional public profile, connect it with communities, and ultimately create a growing network around recognition.

The awards event is the flagship experience, not the entire business.

The Opportunity

Thousands of people and organisations are doing meaningful work, and most of that excellence is invisible outside their immediate networks. Beacon creates a mechanism for discovering and documenting it.

The Beacon Engine

THE FLYWHEELthe last stage feeds back into Discover

We begin upstream, before the nomination reaches the public. Our team actively discovers potential nominees, researches their work, verifies relevance and creates a professional recognition profile before approaching them.

The nominee is not only the subject of recognition. Once participating, they can become part of Beacon's distribution network by sharing their profile and story with their own community.

We don't approach organisations simply because they have money. We approach them because there is a credible connection between what they do and the communities Beacon is building.

The website is Beacon's credibility layer.
